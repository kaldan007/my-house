  • Door and window positions and widths are scaled off image 2. Heights are my defaults (windows 4′ tall on a 3′ sill, doors 7′); change them freely.

  • Ramp (3′ wide, beside the front wall) and steps (5′ wide, outside it) both run north off the veranda. The last step lands at the line of the north wall.
  • Tent roof: ridge sits on the bedroom/living partition line; the veranda continues the living-room slope.
  • Wash basin, toilet, shower and other fittings are left out as asked; only the kitchen counter is shown. It is an L, 2′ deep, running along the north wall all the way to the east wall.
